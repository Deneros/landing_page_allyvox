# Product Overview

VoxAlly is an AI voice agent platform that helps businesses close deals through intelligent voice conversations. The landing page showcases the product's capabilities, pricing, industry solutions, and provides contact information for potential customers.

## Key Features

- AI-powered voice agents for sales conversations
- Multi-industry solutions (various business verticals)
- Integration capabilities with existing business systems
- Real-time analytics and reporting
- Multi-language support (English and Spanish)

## Target Audience

B2B companies looking to automate and enhance their sales processes through AI voice technology.

## Brand Identity

- Primary color: Teal (#2b7a78)
- Dark theme with warm brown tones (#24201c background)
- Professional, modern, tech-forward positioning
- Focus on business outcomes and ROI
